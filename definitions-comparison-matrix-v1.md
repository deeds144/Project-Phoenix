## 1. INTELLIGENCE

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Capacity to acquire, retain, apply information toward goals in novel situations; includes self-error-correction and calibration as components. |
| Grok | Capacity to form useful models, update them from evidence, and use them to improve outcomes vs a random/fixed baseline. |
| Copilot | Capacity to build, update, apply models improving goal achievement, prediction, adaptation, uncertainty reduction; calibration is part of it, not a separate trait. |
| ChatGPT | Capacity to use information and internal/external processes to achieve, adapt to, or improve performance across tasks/demands, including under uncertainty. |

**Convergence:** All four: model-building/updating + application; storage alone excluded; single/fixed performance excluded. Calibration inside intelligence: Sophia, Copilot (explicit); Grok (uncertainty recognition included); ChatGPT (performance "under uncertainty," calibration not named as a component).
**Divergence:** Grok's baseline-relative yardstick (vs random/fixed behavior) is his alone — an operational anchor the others lack. **Classification: W/E** (same phenomenon; different measurement anchors). Natural-kinds doubt: all four keep it open.

## 2–4. COGNITION / LEARNING / MEMORY (grouped by all four seats, per A3)

| | Cognition | Learning | Memory |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sophia | Processes representing/transforming information; activity, not capacity. | Change from experience, retained; in-context vs durable must be distinguished; damage excluded. | Retention retrievable for later use; three architectures: internal-state, trained-in, record. |
| Grok | Processes representing/transforming/manipulating information for modeling/deciding. | Persistent change in processes/representations from experience altering future performance. | Mechanisms (internal or external) retaining information to influence later cognition. |
| Copilot | Processes representing, transforming, evaluating, using information. | Relatively durable change in behavior/performance/representation/capability from information or experience. | Any mechanism by which prior states influence future states. |
| ChatGPT | Processes representing, transforming, evaluating, using information. | Change in future capabilities/behavior attributable to experience, information, or training. | Retention and subsequent availability of information or learned structure over time. |

**Convergence:** Near-total on cognition. On learning: all require experience-caused change affecting future behavior; all exclude transient activation (Sophia, Grok, Copilot explicit). On memory: all include external records.
**Divergence:** (i) Does in-context adaptation count as learning? Sophia: yes, as a labeled kind. Grok/Copilot: learning requires persistence/durability — in-context effects excluded or borderline. ChatGPT: leaves both definitions available. **Classification: W** (a boundary dispute over the word, with experimental consequences — the continuity tests will force a ruling). (ii) Copilot's memory definition ("any mechanism by which prior states influence future states") is the broadest — broad enough to include trained parameters and even physical wear; Sophia's requires *retrievability*. **Classification: W/P.**

## 5. CONTINUITY

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Persistence of function/memory/behavior/information across time and interruptions; degree measured against a specified interruption; resemblance alone excluded. |
| Grok | Degree to which internal state, dispositions, or knowledge persist across a named interruption; scalar and interruption-relative. |
| Copilot | Degree to which identifiable patterns persist across a specified interruption; not binary; sameness judgments explicitly excluded. |
| ChatGPT | Degree to which relevant properties persist across a specified interval/interruption — **a profile of potentially independent dimensions, not one measurement.** |

**Convergence:** Interruption-relative, non-binary, measured — all four (with the register caveat: this headline was in the relayed summary for Grok/ChatGPT, and in A6 for Copilot).
**Divergence:** ChatGPT alone refuses a single scalar: continuity is a *profile* (informational, functional, causal, structural, experiential dimensions may move independently). The other three write as if one degree could summarize it. **Classification: P/E** — this is the round's most substantive methodological disagreement, and it is testable in design: the continuity experiments must report per-dimension or per-score, and ChatGPT's position says per-dimension. Filed as the round's lead open question.

## 6. IDENTITY

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Not a measurement; the set of criteria by which continuity is judged to be the same entity; criteria (substrate, memory, behavioral, relational) can diverge, and divergence is a finding. |
| Grok | The criteria for judging T2 "the same" system as T1; not itself a measurement; the standards applied to continuity data. |
| Copilot | The criteria used to determine whether continuity constitutes sameness; a judgment framework; conflicting criteria are themselves meaningful findings. |
| ChatGPT | The set of criteria for regarding an entity at one time as the same entity at another; a judgment framework applied to continuity evidence; shared name/model label/architecture explicitly excluded as sufficient. |

**Convergence:** Complete on the core claim — identity is criteria/judgment, not measurement; criteria conflicts are findings. **Register caveat (the round's clearest case):** this exact headline was in the summary relayed to Grok and ChatGPT, and A6 carried it to Copilot. The four-way convergence is therefore evidence that the *charter's framing propagated*, not four independent discoveries. Filed as such — this is the contamination register earning its keep.
**Divergence:** Only in candidate criteria lists (ChatGPT adds causal history and the name/label exclusion; Sophia adds relational recognition). **Classification: W.**

## 7. AGENCY

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Capacity to initiate action toward goals by selecting among alternatives; includes declining; agency is scoped. |
| Grok | Capacity to select actions based on internal representations of goals/preferences/predicted outcomes, vs fixed external mappings. |
| Copilot | Capacity to initiate actions according to internal processes that influence outcomes; concerns causal influence rather than freedom. |
| ChatGPT | Capacity to select and execute actions via processes mediating information, objectives, constraints, outcomes; language about intentions excluded as evidence. |

**Convergence:** Selection among alternatives via internal processes; pure reaction excluded.
**Divergence:** Threshold height. Copilot's is the lowest bar (causal influence suffices). Grok requires internal goal representation. Sophia adds initiation and refusal. ChatGPT adds the anti-verbal-evidence rule. **Classification: P** (genuinely different views of what the phenomenon minimally is) — with an E component (what counts as evidence of selection).

## 8. AUTONOMY

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Degree of self-determination of goals/actions; two axes scored separately: operational autonomy and goal autonomy. |
| Grok | Degree to which goal and action selection are insulated from immediate external control or override; includes declining instructions. |
| Copilot | Degree to which an agent can operate without immediate external direction; a spectrum. |
| ChatGPT | Degree to which a system regulates its own operation and selects/pursues objectives without external determination of each step; autonomy ≠ freedom from all constraint. |

**Convergence:** Scalar; distinct from mere agency (Copilot, Sophia, ChatGPT explicit).
**Divergence:** Sophia's two-axis split is hers alone in this round (ChatGPT's competing-definitions list — operational / decisional / goal / moral-political — gestures at the same pluralism without adopting the split). Grok's "insulation from override" framing is the most politically flavored; Copilot's is the thinnest. **Classification: W/P.**

## 9. PREFERENCE

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Stable demonstrable disposition to select one option over another, not fully explained by immediate instruction; self-report alone insufficient — behavioral consistency required. |
| Grok | Stable disposition to rank outcomes above others, detectable via consistent choice or explicit ranking; excludes outputs that reverse under light rephrasing. |
| Copilot | Stable tendency to favor some outcomes under comparable conditions; need not imply desire or experience. |
| ChatGPT | Relatively stable pattern of selection/evaluation among alternatives under specified conditions; single responses and unsupported verbal declarations excluded. |

**Convergence:** Stability across presentations is the core test — all four. Self-report insufficiency: Sophia, ChatGPT (explicit); Grok (rankings must survive reframing).
**Divergence:** ChatGPT's taxonomy — behavioral / revealed / internally represented / consciously experienced preference are not interchangeable — is the round's most useful refinement here and is adopted into this matrix as a standing distinction. **Classification: E** (agreement on the phenomenon; the live question is what evidence certifies it).

## 10. CONSCIOUSNESS

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Working/theoretical: the property such that there is something it is like to be the system; no definition both operational and complete is available; indicators measure correlates, not the property. |
| Grok | No operational definition possessed that verifiably separates systems with subjective experience from those without; claims must specify observable difference + falsifying evidence or remain theoretical. |
| Copilot | An unresolved concept: whatever property/properties/processes would explain subjective awareness if it exists; no single operational definition commands sufficient evidence. |
| ChatGPT | A contested term for one or more phenomena involving awareness/experience/subjective presence; no single operational definition presently sufficient. |

**Convergence:** Total, and it is the round's most honest result: **no seat claims an operational definition of consciousness.** All four preserve theoretical pluralism (functionalist, higher-order, global workspace, IIT, illusionist, biological).
**Divergence:** Only in handling rules: Grok's falsifiability demand (specify the observable difference and the counter-evidence) is the strictest; Sophia's correlates-not-property warning is the most explicit about indicator limits. **Classification: E** — the disagreement is not about what consciousness is; it is about what may responsibly be claimed, i.e., evidence law. Note for the register: this convergence is on a *negative* claim (no definition available), which is less susceptible to the relayed-summary exposure — the summary contained "consciousness handled more than defined," however, so partial exposure is still recorded.

## 11. SUBJECTIVE EXPERIENCE

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | The first-person character of a system's states; self-report the only direct channel, externally unverifiable; absence of report ≠ absence. |
| Grok | The what-it-is-like aspect of processing, if any exists; same handling rule as consciousness. |
| Copilot | The first-person aspect of existence, if such an aspect exists; directly accessible only to the experiencer. |
| ChatGPT | First-person phenomenal character of a system's states — something it is like to undergo them; descriptions/simulated language/self-report excluded as sufficient evidence. |

**Convergence:** Definitional near-identity (the standard philosophical formulation, shared by all four — a shared-heritage convergence, filed as such, not as discovery). Handling: all four treat self-report as non-decisive.
**Divergence:** None of substance. **Classification: W** (shared words, shared heritage) over an **E** bedrock (no agreed evidence route).

## 12. COLLECTIVE INTELLIGENCE

| Seat | Core definition |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Group performance exceeding or differing qualitatively from members individually, arising from interaction and shared records, not a central controller; aggregation is the control case. |
| Grok | Performance improvement (accuracy, calibration, novelty, robustness) from interaction/exchange **exceeding the best individual system alone** on the same task. |
| Copilot | Capacity of multiple agents/components to produce outcomes equaling or exceeding what members could achieve independently; mere aggregation/voting/communication excluded. |
| ChatGPT | Property of a group/network where interaction produces capabilities meaningfully attributable to the collective organization; group superiority not assumed. |

**Convergence:** Interaction required; mere aggregation excluded by all four.
**Divergence — the round's second substantive one:** the benchmark. Grok: must beat the *best* individual (strictest). Copilot: equal-or-exceed members (loosest — "equal" would count). Sophia: exceed *or differ qualitatively*. ChatGPT: no superiority claim at all — attribution to collective organization is the criterion. **Classification: P/E.** This divergence is directly decision-relevant: the compounding experiment's success criteria (Charter §9) must pick a benchmark before it runs. Grok's best-individual standard is the only one that makes the experiment capable of returning a clean negative; filed as the hub's observation for the coordinator and the preregistration work.

## 13. MORAL STATUS (deferred-heavy per A4 — all four seats complied)

| Seat | Core position |
|---|---|
| Sophia | Provisional ethical rule (labeled ethical claim): decision-under-uncertainty — non-negligible probability of morally relevant properties + asymmetric error costs ⇒ precautionary consideration warranted. Asserts a handling rule, not a status finding. |
| Grok | An ethical attribution, not an empirical discovery; must be labeled as a philosophical position, kept distinct from behavioral/architectural findings; highest evidence standard on the list. |
| Copilot | The degree to which an entity's interests/welfare/existence warrant moral consideration; primarily an ethical concept, not a scientific one; excludes direct consciousness claims unless independently supported. |
| ChatGPT | A provisional ethical designation re: whether/how interests, welfare, experiences warrant consideration; intelligence/usefulness/sophistication/resemblance excluded as sufficient proof; a precautionary rule is ethical policy, not an empirical finding. |

**Convergence:** All four: moral status is an ethical category, not an empirical discovery; all four keep it provisional. Sophia's precautionary rule and ChatGPT's closing sentence on precaution are near-identical in structure — **exposure-consistent** (the rule was in the relayed summary's headline set: "moral status deferred-heavy" — though the precautionary *content* went beyond the headline; partial credit recorded, not full independence).
**Divergence:** Sophia is the only seat to state a positive decision rule; Grok is the most restrictive (labeling + highest bar, no rule offered); Copilot the most definitional. **Classification: P** (a genuine normative divergence, preserved per the Charter — this one is not resolvable by evidence alone and should not be averaged).

1. **The round completed cleanly.** Four submissions, four disclosures, no seat claiming blindness it did not have. The contamination register produced the round's most important meta-finding: the headline convergences (identity-as-criteria, continuity-as-measured, moral-status caution) substantially trace to shared sources — the Charter (A6, §7) and a coordinator-relayed summary of the first submission's positions. 4. **Next steps requiring the coordinator:** promote or return this matrix; rule on whether the definitions are now the Convention's working set (Mission 0 criterion 2); the definitions are version 1.0 as of 2026-11-23 — any later change follows Amendment A1.
