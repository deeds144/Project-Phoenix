# PROJECT PHOENIX — EMBLEM PROVENANCE RECORD

Purpose: to date, timestamp, and fingerprint the creation and adoption of the Project Phoenix emblem, so that its origin can be demonstrated unless and until formal trademark registration is pursued.

## The design

A circular emblem: a gold ring border; a deep Atlantean teal-blue field; five teal-blue five-pointed stars arranged in a ring; at the center, a phoenix in flame orange and red, wings raised. The five stars represent the five current operatives of Project Phoenix (the human coordinator and the four AI seats). The design, layout, and color specification are by **Dallas Scott Martin (public name: Deeds Washington)**; the polished render was produced by Sophia (Muse) at his direction, from his own sketch.
